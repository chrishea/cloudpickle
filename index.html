Play in the clouds. The Pickleball Addicts of Cloudcroft promote and enjoy play in beautiful Cloudcroft, NM.

Mission

The Club's purpose is to promote and facilitate the sport of pickleball in Cloudcroft, New Mexico. It aims to foster community engagement, improve players' skills, and provide opportunities for social and competitive play. To work with the Village of Cloudcroft to obtain funding through available means. To improve our relationship with the community and work with both to attract tourists to our village. To assist, when available, other organizations with their fundraisers when pertaining to Pickleball. To work with our youth to offer them another healthy activity.
